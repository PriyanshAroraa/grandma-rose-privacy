Privacy Policy: Grandma Rose: Merge & Story

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Publisher: AanyaLabs (aanyalabs.tech)

Contact: support@aanyalabs.tech

Grandma Rose: Merge & Story ("the game") is a free offline puzzle game. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

  • No account. You do not sign up or log in.
  • No ads and no ad networks. The game does not show ads and does not use an advertising ID.
  • No analytics or tracking. The game does not include analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • No purchases. The game does not sell anything for real money.
  • No network connection needed. The game runs entirely on your device.

What stays on your device

Your progress (level, coins, items on the board, settings) is saved locally on your device so that you can continue later. It never leaves your device. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this saved progress is deleted.

Permissions

  • Internet: declared by the app framework, but the game does not send or receive any of your data.
  • Vibration: used for small touch feedback. You can turn feedback off in Settings.

Children

The game is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, by adding ads or online features), we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before releasing that version.
